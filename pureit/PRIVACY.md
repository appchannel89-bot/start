# PureIt – Privacy Policy

**PureIt - Pip Player** (package `com.pes.pureit`) · Last updated: October 13, 2026

PureIt is a video player made by an independent developer, Đào Sơn Tùng ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **your videos stay on your device, and we do not collect personal data.**

## Videos and files on your device
With your permission (Android "Photos and videos" access), PureIt reads the list of videos stored on your device so it can show them by folder and play them. Videos are played locally. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

When you delete, rename, restore or hide a video, Android asks you to confirm, and the change happens only on your device.

## Private folder
Videos you move to the private folder are copied into the app's private storage on your device and removed from the shared gallery. The folder is protected by a 4-digit PIN, stored only on your device as a salted hash. Private videos and the PIN are excluded from device backups and transfers. If you forget the PIN, the only way to reset it is to delete the private folder's contents; we cannot recover it for you.

## Information stored on your device
The app keeps your settings, the position where you stopped each video (so it can resume) and the last video you watched. This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Network use
- **Network streams:** if you enter a video link, the app connects to that address to play it.
- **Chromecast:** if you cast a video, the app uses Google Cast to find devices on your Wi-Fi network and serves the video directly from your phone to your TV over your local network. Google Cast is provided by Google and covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); Google Play services may collect diagnostic information needed for casting to work.

## What we do not do
- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no analytics SDKs.
- We do not sell or share personal data.

## Children
PureIt is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Open-source components
Video playback is powered by libVLC from the VideoLAN project, licensed under the LGPL 2.1.
